Official document · 2025 · budget allocation

2025 Citizens' Budget: roads, education and health

The state budget lists ₦125.97bn for rural and riverine roads, ₦123.03bn for highways and urban roads, ₦32bn for higher education and ₦30.02bn for health. These are allocations, not proof of expenditure.

How this website handles claims

Every entry should identify the person or institution responsible, location, date, project stage, source link and last-checked date. Budgeted does not mean spent. Flagged-off does not mean completed. Government claims should be attributed to the government; independent verification should be clearly identified.
